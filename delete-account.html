Deleting your Rummage account

Last updated: 12 September 2026

Rummage is a receipt scanner. Signing in is optional — you can scan without an account — and an account exists only to count your monthly scans against you rather than against one phone.

Delete from inside the app

1. Open Rummage 2. Go to Settings 3. Tap Delete my account 4. Confirm

The deletion happens immediately. You are signed out straight away.

If you no longer have the app

Reinstall Rummage, sign in with the same Google account, and use the button above. That is the reliable route, and it is worth saying why rather than offering something that sounds easier and does not work.

The only thing we store for your account is the identifier Google gives us - not your email address, not your name. An email from you therefore gives us nothing we can match against a record. Signing in proves which record is yours; an email cannot.

If you genuinely cannot do that - no access to the Google account, or the app will not run on any device you have - email sales.quoteback@gmail.com and say so. We will work out with you what can be verified, and confirm the outcome within 30 days. We would rather tell you honestly that a record cannot be located than delete somebody else's.

What is deleted

Everything we hold for your account, which is one record:

  • Your Google account identifier
  • How many scans you have used, and in which month

That is the entire contents of your account. It is deleted permanently and immediately, not marked for later removal, and there is no backup copy that outlives it.

What is not deleted, and why

Your receipts. They are not ours to delete — they have only ever been stored on your phone. Deleting your account does not touch them. To remove them, use Erase all data in Settings, or uninstall the app.

Your scan allowance for the current month. Scans are counted twice: once against your account, and once against the phone. Deleting the account clears the first. The second is kept, so that deleting and recreating an account cannot be used to reset the free monthly limit. That device count is not linked to you or to any account - it is a keyed cryptographic fingerprint of a device identifier, readable back only with a key we hold and never shared - and it is discarded when the month ends.

A subscription, if you have one. Deleting your Rummage account does not cancel it. Subscriptions are billed by Google Play or Apple, not by us, and only they can stop a renewal - so if you want the charges to stop, cancel in your app store's subscription settings as well. We would rather say this plainly than let anyone delete an account believing it ended a payment.

Anonymous usage events. Events like "a scan started" are grouped by a random installation identifier that is not connected to your account. Erasing all data in Settings issues a new one. To have the existing events deleted, email us the Installation ID shown at the bottom of Settings.
